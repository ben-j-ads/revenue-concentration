# Revenue Concentration

If your biggest customers left, how much would you lose? Enter three numbers from last year: total revenue, how many customers bought, and how much your 10 biggest customers spent. The page shows how much of your revenue rests on that small group.

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step, no dependencies, no network calls.

- **Verdict:** whether a few customers carry the business.
- **Shows:** the top-10 share of revenue, the top-10 share of customers, and everyone else's revenue.
- **Arithmetic:** share = revenue from top 10 / total revenue. The full formulas are on the page.
- **Share:** a paper-ground PNG card, and the URL hash carries your numbers.

Example figures are made up. Use one full year and net revenue.
