do a few customers carry you?

If your biggest customers left, how much would you lose? Enter three numbers from last year and see how much of your revenue rests on a handful of people.

Last year, roughly

Your 10 biggest customers are the ten people or businesses who spent the most with you. Your sales report or customer list can sort them. Example numbers are made up.

How it works

The arithmetic
share from top 10 = revenue from top 10 / total revenue top 10 as share of customers = 10 / number of customers

Plain version: divide the revenue from your 10 biggest customers by everything you sold. The bigger that share, the more you depend on a few people.

Read this before you act on the number

Use one full year. A single month can make a regular buyer look like a big one, or a one-off look like a loyal customer.

Use net revenue. Refunds should come off the top customers before you add them up.

There is no good or bad percentage. Some businesses should depend on a few big accounts. The question is whether you know who they are and are looking after them.
